# Smart Dynamic Pricing Engine for E-Commerce

This project implements a dynamic pricing engine for e-commerce platforms that adjusts product prices in real-time based on various market factors and business rules.

## Overview

The Smart Dynamic Pricing Engine helps e-commerce businesses optimize their pricing strategy by automatically adjusting prices based on:

- Market demand and supply
- Competitor pricing
- Inventory levels
- Customer behavior and segmentation
- Time-based factors (seasonality, time of day)
- Business objectives (profit maximization, market share, etc.)
